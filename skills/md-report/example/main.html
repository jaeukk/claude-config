Key results

  • Stealthy patterns have no scattering for probes with $2k\le K$, until the sample is too thick.
  • Crystallization sets in around χ ∼ 0.5.

The notes

The transparency window

The window closes once the sample is too thick, $L/\ell_s^0\ll k_r\ell_s^0$.

Thickness
